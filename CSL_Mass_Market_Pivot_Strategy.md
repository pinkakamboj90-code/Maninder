# CSL — Mass Market Pivot Strategy

## The Strategic Insight

> Pure cognitive science = small audience.
> Viral self-improvement hooks + cognitive science as the credibility layer = mass audience + premium ads.

This is exactly how **Andrew Huberman** built a 6M+ subscriber empire. He doesn't title videos "Mesolimbic Dopaminergic Pathways." He titles them **"Controlling Your Dopamine for Motivation, Focus & Satisfaction."** Same science. Mass-market hook.

This is how **Ali Abdaal** scaled to 6.1M subscribers. He doesn't lecture on cognitive load theory. He says **"How to Stop Procrastinating and Finally Take Action."** The science is inside. The title sells.

## INSPIRATION DECK — WHAT TO COPY (AND WHY)

### From Ali Abdaal
- **Hook style:** Personal frame ("How I...")
- **Title formula:** Outcome + simplicity ("...in 90 Days")
- **Thumbnail:** Bold text + facial expression (we'll do bold text + abstract icon)
- **Steal this:** *"How to Change Your Life in 90 Days"* -> Adapt to *"The 90-Day Brain Reset (Backed by Neuroscience)"*

### From Andrew Huberman
- **Hook style:** Mechanism + benefit ("Controlling Your Dopamine for...")
- **Authority moves:** Citing studies, peer-reviewed research, lab terminology
- **Length:** 1-3 hour deep dives (we'll do 10-min versions)
- **Steal this:** *"Controlling Your Dopamine for Motivation, Focus & Satisfaction"* -> Adapt to *"The Dopamine System That Controls Your Discipline (10-Minute Breakdown)"*

### From Alex Hormozi
- **Hook style:** Brutal honesty + reframe ("Force Your Brain to Crave Hard Things")
- **Tone:** Direct, no fluff, repetition for emphasis
- **Steal this:** *"How to Force Your Brain to Crave Doing Hard Things"* -> Adapt to *"How to Make Your Brain Crave the Work It Hates"*

### From Tim Urban (TED — Procrastination)
- **Hook style:** Universal relatability + humour + science
- **Why it has 60M+ views:** Made procrastination feel *seen*
- **Steal this approach:** Use illustrations of "instant gratification monkey" style metaphors — the rational brain vs. the lazy brain framing.

### From Tiago Forte
- **Hook style:** Systems & frameworks (PARA, CODE)
- **Selling angle:** "Stop wasting your best ideas"
- **Steal this:** Frameworks are share-bait. Every CSL video should end with a 3-rule or 4-rule framework viewers can screenshot.
