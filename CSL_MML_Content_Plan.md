## Channel Overview

### Cognitive Strategy Lab (CSL)
- **Platform:** Faceless YouTube + Facebook Page
- **Niche:** Psychology, cognitive performance, learning science, behavioural economics
- **Target Audience:** Western (UK/US) professionals, students, lifelong learners
- **Tagline:** "Structured Systems for Smarter Learning"
- **Tone:** Sharp professor meets psychologist. Mechanism-driven, evidence-referenced.
- **Visual Identity:** Dark navy + gold | Cormorant Garamond + Montserrat
- **Video Length:** Up to 10 minutes (Phase 1) | 15–18 minutes (Phase 2)
- **Format:** Scripted voiceover, no talking head, all assets built from scratch

### Money Mind Lab (MML)
- **Platform:** Facebook Page + Future YouTube Channel
- **Niche:** Behavioural economics & money psychology
- **Target Audience:** UK/US adults interested in finance psychology
- **Tone:** Analytical, slightly editorial. Not personal finance advice — psychology of money behaviour.
- **Visual Identity:** Brutalist Financial Editorial — deep forest green + electric lime
- **Video Length:** Up to 10 minutes (Phase 1) | 15–18 minutes (Phase 2)
- **Format:** Series-based carousels (Facebook), scripted voiceover videos (YouTube)
