## 12. Production Recommendations

You said: *MacBook Pro + iPad + iPhone + Adobe Creative Cloud + light Premiere/After Effects experience.* Here's the honest, specific production stack.

### Recording (Voiceover)
- **Mic:** If you have one, great. If not, **Shure MV7+** (USB-C, plug into Mac, sounds broadcast-quality, ~£250). You can also start with iPhone 15 Pro on a stand using an app like *Ferrite* — quality is genuinely good for the first 20 videos.
- **Recording app:** **Adobe Audition** (already in your CC). Use the *Adaptive Noise Reduction*, *DeEsser* (-25dB cut), and *Multiband Compressor*. One template, save it, reuse it. Skip if it intimidates you for now and use Premiere directly.
- **AI fallback:** **ElevenLabs** with a British male voice (Cognitive Strategy Lab feels like a British-academic-meets-podcaster tone). MML can use the same voice or a slightly more editorial American male. Both channels are at the level where AI voice now sounds 95% human.

### Editing (Premiere Pro)
- **Template approach:** Build **one master Premiere template per channel** with title cards, lower thirds, transition stack, end card. Reuse for every video. This kills 50% of your editing time.
- **Workflow:** Voiceover first → cut filler/silences with **Premiere's text-based editing** (this feature alone saves hours) → drop B-roll/motion graphics on top → music bed → export.
- **Time per video (after first 3):** 3–4 hours, not 8–10.

### Motion Graphics (After Effects)
- You don't need pro AE skills. You need **3 templates**:
  1. **Number reveal animation** (for stats: "44%", "£10,000", "23 minutes")
  2. **Quote card animation** (citation reveals — Kahneman, Ericsson, Sweller)
  3. **Concept diagram animation** (forgetting curve, dopamine cycle, herd graph)
- Buy a single AE template pack on **Envato Elements** (£12.99/month) — use the same 3 templates across 100 videos. Nobody notices. Kurzgesagt reuses motifs constantly.

### Visuals
- **Stock:** Envato Elements covers all your stock motion + sounds for one fee.
- **Custom illustrations:** Use **Midjourney** or **DALL-E 3** for your channel-specific style frames. Train a consistent visual prompt for each channel:
  - **CSL:** "Dark navy background, gold accent, isometric brain illustration, academic editorial style, clean, no text"
  - **MML:** "Brutalist financial editorial, deep forest green, electric lime accent, geometric finance illustrations, no text"
- Stay consistent — your audience should recognise your visual language in 2 seconds.

### Thumbnails
- **App:** Photoshop (you have it) or **Figma** (free) — Figma is faster.
- **Rule:** One image, 3–4 words MAX, one bright accent colour. No clutter. Look at MrBeast and Veritasium thumbnails — they nail this.
- **Test:** Make 3 thumbnails per video, A/B test using YouTube's built-in thumbnail testing (now free for everyone since Q4 2025).
