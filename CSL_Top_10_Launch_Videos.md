# CSL — Top 10 Launch Videos (Beginner-Friendly, Maximum Performance)

## Why These 10 Videos?

I picked these based on 5 criteria:
1. **Proven viral demand** — similar titles have 1M+ views across YouTube
2. **Easy to produce** — you can script, record, and edit each in 1–2 days
3. **Short & punchy** — 7–8 minutes max (shorter = easier to make, better retention at start)
4. **High search volume** — people actively search these topics daily
5. **Premium advertisers** — SaaS, EdTech, productivity apps, finance all bid on these keywords

## PRODUCTION NOTES FOR BEGINNERS

Since you're starting out, keep it simple:

| Element | Recommendation |
|---------|---------------|
| **Script length** | 1,000–1,200 words per video (= 7–8 min at 155 wpm) |
| **Editing style** | Simple cuts, text on screen, stock motion graphics |
| **Music** | Ambient/lo-fi underneath (use Epidemic Sound or Artlist) |
| **Voiceover** | Use ElevenLabs (British male, calm, authoritative) |
| **Recording time** | 30 min per script (with retakes) |
| **Editing time** | 2–4 hours per video max |
| **Output target** | 2 videos per week |
| **Batch workflow** | Write 2 scripts Sunday → Record Monday → Edit Tue/Wed → Upload Thu/Mon |
